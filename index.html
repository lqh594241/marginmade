THE BUSINESS BITS

Beautiful things deserve healthy margins.

01 / KNOW YOUR COST

Your time belongs in the calculation.

Labor = hands-on minutes ÷ 60 × your hourly rate. Add materials, packaging and actual shipping. Customer-paid shipping is revenue, not a reduction of the cost.

02 / SET REAL FEES

No two marketplaces are quite alike.

Enter the percentage and fixed fees that apply to your account. Platform and ad fees use item revenue plus customer shipping; payment fees also include the tax you collect.

03 / PRICE FOR MARGIN

Markup and margin are different things.

Profit margin = profit ÷ revenue, excluding collected tax. The suggested price works backward from your target margin and is rounded upward to cents.

GOOD QUESTIONS, MAKER

Let’s clear
a few things up.

Practical answers for your next pricing decision.

Are these the current Etsy fees?

No. The default fee rates are examples and are not maintained as official marketplace pricing. Enter your actual regional, account, payment and advertising charges.

Does the suggested price account for discounts?

Yes. It calculates a pre-discount item price that reaches your target margin after the entered discount, shipping, costs and fees, under the stated fee bases.

How is collected tax handled?

Tax is treated as collected and fully remitted, so it is not income or profit. The payment percentage fee includes it. This does not model VAT-inclusive prices, tax credits or jurisdiction-specific tax obligations.

What does quantity mean?

It represents identical separate single-item orders. Each order has its own shipping and fixed fees. This is not a multi-item basket or combined-shipping calculator.
